# SEO titles and meta descriptions per brand (T1, 2026-10-09)

Ready to paste into each brand site and its order page. Source: brand config copy (`GBP_CHANGES.md`) and the findings in
`PRESENCE.md`. Rules: title <= 60 characters, meta description <= 155 characters (longer gets cut in Google results),
brand name first in the title, real menu words plus the neighbourhood in every line, the counter-price pickup promise
where it fits, **no hours** (not final) and never "24/7". Lengths below are checked by script.

Order page (`/commander` FR, `/order` EN) of every brand, same pattern:
- Title FR: `Commander <Marque> | Cueillette et livraison` · EN: `Order <Brand> | Pickup and delivery`
- Meta FR: `Commandez <Marque> en ligne : cueillette au prix du comptoir, ou livraison par l'application de votre choix.`
- Meta EN: `Order <Brand> online: pickup at the counter price, or delivery through the app you prefer.`

Names marked "waits for the owner" may still change (`PLAN.md` §7, group D): update the line when they do.
