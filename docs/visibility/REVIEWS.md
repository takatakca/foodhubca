# Review kit (Q1, 2026-10-09)

How every brand asks for reviews without breaking a platform's rules. Reply templates are in `GBP_CHANGES.md`.

> **Pour le propriétaire.** On demande un avis Google à **tous** les clients, de la même façon, sans cadeau ni rabais.
> On ne filtre jamais (pas « si vous êtes content, laissez un avis »). Sur Yelp, on ne demande rien du tout.

## 1. The rules (per platform)

| Platform | Ask customers? | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Google | Yes: the same request to every customer | Ask only happy customers ("review gating"), give a reward or discount, ask staff or family, set staff quotas, ask for specific words or stars, pressure people at the counter |
| Yelp | **No.** Yelp forbids asking for reviews in any form and filters or penalizes solicited ones. A neutral "Find us on Yelp" is the most to show | "Review us on Yelp", surveys that send happy customers to Yelp, review vendors |
| TripAdvisor | Yes, neutrally, to everyone | Rewards, filtering, writing reviews for the business |
| Uber Eats, DoorDash | The apps ask for ratings themselves | Offering anything in exchange for a rating |

Breaking these rules gets reviews removed and can restrict the profile. In Canada, fake or rewarded reviews can also be
deceptive marketing.

## 2. Getting each profile's review link

- **Google:** once the profile is verified, its "Ask for reviews" / "Get more reviews" button gives a short link. One
  link per brand **per kitchen** (each kitchen has its own profile). Save it in Drive 02 APPROVED_VALUES next to the
  profile, never in this public repo if it carries private ids.
- **TripAdvisor:** from the owner's management center, once the listing is claimed.
- **Yelp:** no review link is used (rule above).
- Make one QR code per Google review link. Test it with two phones before printing.

## 3. Bag card (one per brand and kitchen, in every bag: counter and app orders)

Size: a business card or a quarter page. Same card for every customer.

**FR**
> Merci d'avoir choisi **<Marque>** !
> Votre avis aide notre petite cuisine de **<quartier>**. Laissez-nous un avis sur Google : **[QR]**
> Commandez la prochaine fois au prix du comptoir : **<lien de la page de commande>**

**EN**
> Thank you for choosing **<Brand>**!
> Your review helps our small **<area>** kitchen. Leave us a Google review: **[QR]**
> Next time, order at the counter price: **<order page link>**

Not on the card: rewards ("10% off for a review"), "5 stars" requests, "if you're happy... / if not, contact us" (that
is filtering), hours (not final), "24/7", phone numbers of people.

## 4. Other moments to ask (same neutral words)

- Counter: a small sign by the pickup shelf with the QR ("Votre avis compte / Your review matters").
- Order confirmation page and pickup receipt: one line with the review link.
- Google posts: once a month, a neutral "Tell us how we did" post.
- Never in a private message to only the customers who praised the food.

## 5. Replying

Reply to every review within 48 hours with the templates in `GBP_CHANGES.md`, adapted to each review (never the same
text pasted on every review). A complaint gets an apology, one concrete fix and a way to reach the kitchen through the
order page; never argue, never share the customer's order details in public.

Sources: Google's prohibited "fake engagement" content (discouraging negative reviews or selectively soliciting
positive ones), summarized at https://www.podium.com/article/review-gating ; Yelp's "don't ask for reviews" rule,
summarized at https://searchengineland.com/yelp-increasingly-cracking-review-solicitation-across-internet-286290 and
https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/review-management/dos-and-donts/review-platform-guidelines/ . Check each platform's
own help page before printing, as wording changes.
