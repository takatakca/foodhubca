# Photo shot list per brand (P2, 2026-10-09)

What to photograph for each brand, once, then reuse everywhere (Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp, the apps, Facebook,
Instagram, the brand site and order page). The owner's existing photos are in OneDrive; this session cannot open them
(the Microsoft 365 connector is not connected), so first sort the OneDrive photos against this list and shoot only what
is missing.

> **Pour le propriétaire.** Pour chaque marque : 1 logo, 1 photo de couverture, 5 plats, l'emballage, la cuisine.
> De vraies photos de cette marque seulement, jamais l'emballage d'une autre marque dans la photo.

## Rules (all brands)

- **Real food from this brand only.** No stock photos, no AI-generated dishes, no photo of another brand's food.
- **One brand per photo.** The kitchen makes many brands: keep other brands' bags, boxes, stickers and menus out of the
  frame. Google checks that delivery brands have their own packaging.
- **People:** no faces without written consent; no customer names, order tickets or screens in the frame.
- **Light:** daylight or a soft lamp, plain background, the dish filling most of the frame; one top view and one 45°
  view per dish.
- **No text on photos** for Google (logos and prices on images look like ads).

## Sizes (shoot big once, crop for each place)

Shoot every photo at least 3000 px wide, landscape. Then crop:

| Use | Crop | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile photos | 1:1 or 4:3, at least 720 x 720 | JPG or PNG, 10 KB to 5 MB (Google's minimum is 250 x 250) |
| Google logo | 1:1 | Square, the logo centred on a plain background |
| Google cover | 16:9 | The brand's best dish |
| Delivery apps (menu items, header) | 16:9 or 3:2, at least 1600 px wide | Check each app's own photo guide before uploading |
| Instagram | 1:1 (1080 x 1080) or 4:5 (1080 x 1350) | |
| Facebook cover | wide (about 1640 x 856) | Keep the dish in the centre; the sides get cut on phones |
| Brand site / order page hero | 16:9, 1920 px wide | Compress to under 300 KB |

## File names

`<brand>-<kitchen>-<subject>-<yyyymmdd>.jpg`, lowercase, no accents, for example
`ocrepe-saint-leonard-crepe-nutella-20261015.jpg`. Keep the masters in OneDrive in one folder per brand; never put
photos with faces or tickets in this public repo.
