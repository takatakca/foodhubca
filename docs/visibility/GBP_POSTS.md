Eight weekly posts per brand, after the first four in `GBP_CHANGES.md`. FR first, EN under it (post both if the profile
shows two languages; otherwise FR). Each post: one real photo of the dish named (`PHOTOS.md`), button "Commander /
Order" to the brand's order page (`ORDER_HUB.md`). No hours, no prices, no promotions that are not real, never
"24/7". **Kitchen:** each Google profile is one kitchen; where a line shows `[NDG | Saint-Léonard]`, keep only the
kitchen of the profile you post on. Dishes come from the brand config copy; if a dish leaves the menu, skip its post. Brands marked "waits" get no
posts until the owner decides (`PLAN.md` §7).
