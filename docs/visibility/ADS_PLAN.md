## Gates before the first ad (all must be true)

1. The brand's stores are live on the apps it links to, and its own order page works.
2. Its Google Business Profile is verified and correct (`GBP_APPLY.md`) and linked to the Ads account.
3. Hours are approved (ads run only while the kitchen takes orders).
4. Conversion tracking works: a click on "Commander / Order" (own page or app button) and, when possible, a completed
   pickup order. Test with one real order before spending.
5. The brand's name is settled (`PLAN.md` §7, group D). Brands still waiting are not advertised.

## Structure

- **One Search campaign per kitchen** (NDG, Saint-Léonard), one ad group per brand. Search is the most controllable at a
  small budget; Performance Max only later, once each kitchen has 30+ conversions a month.
- **Location:** a radius around the kitchen, starting at 3 km (only where the kitchen really delivers), option
  "Presence: people in or regularly in the area".
- **Languages:** French and English. French ads are required in Québec; English ads run alongside, never alone.
- **Schedule:** the approved hours only, with more weight in the evening. Never "24/7".
- **Budget:** the owner's small daily plan (the $1/day idea per brand to confirm). Start with Maximize clicks and a
  cost-per-click cap; switch to Maximize conversions after about 30 conversions.
- **Keywords:** `KEYWORDS.md`, phrase match, one brand per term per area (overlap table there); the negative list there
  on every campaign; the brand's own name as a keyword in its own ad group.
- **Landing page:** the brand's order page (`ORDER_HUB.md`), never an app page directly.
- **Assets:** location (from the Business Profile), sitelinks (Menu, Order, Pickup), callout "Cueillette au prix du
  comptoir / Pickup at the counter price", images from `PHOTOS.md`.
- **Never:** competitors' names as keywords or in ads, prices that differ from the menu, "best" claims, "24/7", Po
  Poulet NDG.
